DARIVO / STUDIO 01

Three ways to
feel a place.

A concept study for a private property office. Each direction explores an arrival and a living map in English and Arabic. These are interactive design studies with clearly marked demo content.

01 / NOIR CINEMATIC

A life, well placed.

Atmosphere and quiet scale. A map becomes the stage for a private conversation.

02 / SAND EDITORIAL

The considered atlas.

Architectural restraint. Geography and evidence arranged like a personal journal.

03 / GLASS ATLAS

A clearer perspective.

A spatial instrument. Translucent context and an emerging brief over a living map.
