# Legal and operational review before real use

This is a synthetic local demonstration. It does **not** claim compliance with UAE PDPL, Egypt's Data Protection Law (Law 151/2020), real-estate licensing rules, immigration rules, or financial-advice regulations. The following requires qualified legal, security and operational review.

- Establish the UAE company's legal entity, controller/processor roles, jurisdictions and lawful purposes.
- Review UAE PDPL and Egypt Law 151/2020 applicability, implementing rules, regulator guidance, registration and cross-border transfer obligations.
- Confirm hosting, backups, logs and subprocessors physically match approved data regions. `DATA_REGION` is configuration intent only.
- Approve bilingual privacy notices, informed consent, retention schedules, access/export/correction/deletion procedures and breach response.
- Establish invitation, identity verification, staff access reviews, least privilege, session revocation and off-market consent policies. No real KYC provider is integrated.
- Review listing advertising requirements, developer permissions, permit verification, source licensing and media rights. Synthetic permit values must never be presented as official permits.
- Validate Golden Visa thresholds, residency criteria and disclaimers with qualified UAE counsel before showing any real-world eligibility suggestion. The demo's configurable AED 2 million indicator is illustrative.
- Review financial estimate assumptions, FX licensing/freshness, advertising language and advisor handoff boundaries. No tax, legal or personal investment advice is provided.
- Review AI vendor contracts, data processing/retention, residency, prompt logging, third-party transfer consent and model evaluation. External mode is opt-in.
- Provision a real mail adapter with secure delivery and verified sender identity; enforce staff MFA/SSO before real deployment.
- Replace the single-instance rate limiter with a distributed implementation; add monitoring, backups, restore drills, key rotation, vulnerability management and penetration testing.
- Review immutable/minimized audit retention separately from client deletion and ensure incident-investigation needs are justified.
- Commission human Arabic language, keyboard, screen-reader and WCAG 2.2 AA reviews. Automated axe scans are useful checks, not certification.
- Verify real PostgreSQL 16 deployment and CI in the destination repository. Local PGlite verification is not a production infrastructure audit.
