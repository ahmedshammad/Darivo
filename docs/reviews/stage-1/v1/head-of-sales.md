**Gate: revise before advancing.** The shortcomings are small in surface area but belong to the reusable foundation, so they should not be multiplied across future client and developer screens.

## Three delights

1. **The visual language gives a residence room to matter.** The three project-card sizes in the [desktop guide](../media/v2/design-en-dark-1440.png) establish different editorial roles instead of forcing every project into an identical listing tile. The source and Demo vocabulary remain available at each scale.
2. **Arabic receives the same level of presentation.** [Arabic mobile arrival](../media/v2/design-ar-light-390-arrival.png) and the [Arabic sheet](../media/v2/signature-ar-sheet.png) show an intentional typographic hierarchy. This looks like the beginning of a bilingual private office, not an English presentation reflected across the screen.
3. **Illustration is not being passed off as evidence.** The payment plan is explicitly a demo projection, the fixed FX basis is visible, and source/date/basis components exist alongside it. The [native data evidence](../native-db.json) preserves real organisations and reference projects separately from 250 fictional units. That protects developer identity before any actual brand enters a showcase.

## Three problems

1. **The numeral control does not consistently deliver its promise.** The [interaction check](../media/v2/critic-interactions.json) changes the monetary figures to Arabic-Indic digits while leaving area and delivery years in Latin numerals. A client can read both, but a deliberate preference should be deliberate throughout the specimen.
2. **The keyboard shortcut feels less finished than the visual system.** Opening the command palette lands on Close, and typing does not search until the input is separately focused. Its first ArrowUp movement is also incorrect. This is not the first interaction I would want a detail-oriented client to discover during a presentation.
3. **A mobile comparison should reveal that there is another residence.** The [390px evidence view](../media/v2/signature-en-evidence.png) shows only the first concept and a sliver of the next column. Keep the financial table, but make its sideways movement more apparent. This refinement would help an advisor explain alternatives without coaching the client through the interface.

## Scope and judgment

The [capture manifest](../media/v2/manifest.json) reports clean automated accessibility, overflow and browser-error checks. The independent keyboard findings are narrower, observed defects that still need correction. Correct local EGP milestone arithmetic and focus return from the sheet support the positive scores. No native-language, legal, live-inventory or physical-device certification is implied.

**Verdict:** “This foundation could carry a serious developer presentation. Resolve the interaction details now, while they are still one shared component rather than a repeated client frustration.”
