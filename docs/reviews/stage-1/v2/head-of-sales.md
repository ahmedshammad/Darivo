## Three remaining refinements

1. **A future flagship needs a distinct selected state.** The three component sizes are a sound foundation. Preserve room for the chosen project's identity as real stories arrive; avoid flattening every project into the same arrangement merely because the reusable card is convenient.
2. **Show uncertainty as elegantly as the clean reference example.** A companion source specimen with an undated or low-confidence record would prepare the presentation vocabulary for the actual reference dataset. Do not turn unknown dates into precise ones just to complete the layout.
3. **The horizontal comparison remains an advisor teaching moment on mobile.** The new hint is a clear improvement. Retaining the current concept name while scrolling would make side-by-side discussion easier, without sacrificing the factual table.
