## A — “A private tone, but not enough context.”

Three delights: the opening avoids a form grid; one recommendation receives deliberate attention; a Brief has a persistent place. Three problems: the current Map view uses an assumed family/water brief before meaningful input; the geography under the mobile card is hard to reference; the initial Arabic location caption remains English. Verdict: useful atmosphere, but a client should not need me to explain the interface.

## B — “The most readable handover vocabulary.”

Three delights: shortlist entries have a consistent reading order; save makes an explicit selection; the composer sits next to the resulting context. Three problems: the map retains unrelated cover-story copy; the mobile Home sheet handle appears before a selection exists; the active map location can fall behind the sheet. Verdict: borrow the reading order, not the page structure.

## C — “The conversation and Brief finally share the same place.”

Three delights: the Brief stays visible on desktop; two markets share one entry point; the demonstration accurately says nothing is saved. Three problems: the mobile Brief needs an equally discoverable home; the same reasoning across three cards does not help me understand preference; map labels sit under navigation and the hero copy. Verdict: choose this structure and resolve mobile context before advancing.
