**Gate judgment: passes this persona's Stage 0 concept review.** None of these scores means “perfect.” The remaining refinements below keep this at nine rather than ten. Other persona failures must still be resolved before the stage can advance.

## Three delights

1. **The cross-market proposition is unmistakable.** [Desktop Home](../media/v4/c-home-en-dark-1440.png) treats Egypt and the UAE as two parts of one client relationship. The cleaner globe is more persuasive than the earlier mass of country labels, and the understated cards respect the geography rather than competing with it.
2. **The presentation now distinguishes residences instead of just renaming them.** [Tablet Map](../media/v4/c-map-en-light-1024.png) gives the fictional options different reasons and limitations. The Demo badges remain in place, and the real reference sits behind a separately labelled action. This is the correct boundary between a design demonstration and a real developer claim.
3. **A client can inspect the evidence without losing the conversation.** The [Arabic source panel](../media/v4/c-source-ar-light-390-review.png) has a visible listing basis and date, source links, approximate-position disclosure and an explicit distinction from demo concepts. The tested Escape return and Edit focus preserve context. [Arabic mobile Map](../media/v4/c-map-ar-light-390.png) now retains Egypt as an immediately available destination.

## Three remaining problems

1. **The real reference needs a more identifiable citation label.** In the [source panel](../media/v4/c-source-ar-light-390-review.png), two links look like the same publisher destination. A document/listing title would help a buyer distinguish the supporting records before opening them.
2. **The opportunity for individual identity is still mostly in the prose.** The fictional recommendations in [Map](../media/v4/c-map-en-light-1440.png) now differ meaningfully, but their visual weight is identical. During the next design-system work, preserve a generous selected-state treatment so a future flagship can feel singular without introducing a property-card grid. This is a refinement direction, not a demand for Stage 5 features now.
3. **The geographic reveal remains the slowest part of the invitation.** The [local measurements](../media/v4/quality.json) put map readiness as high as 3.58 seconds. The input and content arrive promptly enough for this sales persona's concept demonstration, but that upper tail should shrink before clients are shown an implemented experience on mobile networks.

## Why these scores changed

V1's missing North Coast control, crowded globe, repetitive reasons and weak attribution have been visibly corrected. The Amiri editorial treatment in [Arabic Home](../media/v4/c-home-ar-dark-1440.png), visible attribution in [mobile Map](../media/v4/c-map-ar-light-390.png), and source disclosure give the concept greater brand credibility. The score increase is tied to those repairs, not to a promise of future functionality.

The [quality report](../media/v4/quality.json) supplies zero recorded Home/Map axe violations or overflow across 12 configurations, correct school-intent treatment, and a smooth local moving-frame sample. I score the observed local concept experience; I do not infer 4G, native-reader, screen-reader or physical-phone certification. The [independent keyboard evidence](../media/v4/critic-interactions.json) supports dialog dismissal, sheet operation and focus return.

**Verdict:** “I would take this direction into a private launch design meeting. It respects the places, the buyer and the distinction between evidence and illustration.”
