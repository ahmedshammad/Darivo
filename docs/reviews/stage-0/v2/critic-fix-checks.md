## Quality evidence interpretation

- Inspect all requested **24 static states**: two views × two languages × two themes × three widths. A few attractive desktop frames cannot stand in for the matrix.
- Review recorded movement for geographic continuity, correct selection, readable state changes and interaction that remains usable during camera motion. No automatic repeated flights or motion that blocks input.
- `quality.json` must identify browser/runtime, viewport, sampling method and any warm-cache/local-server conditions. Local LCP and render-frame median/p95 support a **local prototype** speed/motion judgment. They do not prove 4G LCP, physical mid-range phone performance or sustained production 60fps.
- Inspect axe results for the stated 12 configurations. Any serious/critical violation or untested primary state must be resolved or recorded as open. Zero automated violations does not establish WCAG 2.2 AA on its own.
- Verify visible keyboard focus, Enter submission, destination activation, Brief editing and sheet expansion/collapse. Reduced motion must suppress camera animation rather than merely shorten a CSS transition.
- Confirm no horizontal overflow and usable primary target sizes at390. Ensure the user can return to the map after opening more context. A mobile screen may intentionally simplify detail; it must not silently remove a market.
- Treat a tile/network failure as a meaningful state: text, composer and destination controls should remain legible, and the map should not silently pretend to be loaded. This is a graceful prototype limitation, not a requirement to build an offline tile system in Stage 0.

## Persona judgment after checks

**The Critic:** Does the concept now have one strong organising idea, a clear first action, quiet geographic evidence and an equally composed mobile/Arabic experience? It must not solve clutter by layering more panels. A repaired defect removes an objection; it does not automatically earn nine.

**Head of Sales:** Does the concept make each fictional residence feel individually considered while explaining the real two-market proposition? Do source and Demo treatments protect developer identity? A strong Stage 0 Developer Appeal score means the direction merits further development; it is not proof of qualified leads or an approved launch presentation.

Each persona will assign all twelve scores from the new evidence, record three delights and three remaining problems, and give a one-line verdict. Any remaining material issue may keep the stage below the required gate. Future-stage implementation omissions will be listed as scope limits, not scored as current concept defects.
