## Why advance Noir Atlas

The arrival has a distinctive geographic proposition: two markets become one private-office conversation. The chosen direction now sustains that proposition across responsive EN/AR layouts instead of collapsing into a filter grid. The v1 failures have concrete repairs: quiet globe labeling, Noir/Sand palette, upright Amiri display text, visible mobile targets, all market controls, differentiated reasons, an accessible expandable reply, clear provenance, readable attribution and retained multi-market intent. V3's additional lost-context and wrong-reference defects were fixed in v4 and independently exercised.

## Required production follow-through

1. **Map arrival:** provide an immediate geographic preview/loading composition and progressive interactive map. Measure cold/warm readiness separately from text LCP, and verify the specified network and physical-device budgets. The Critic's speed objection remains open for Stage2.
2. **Reference clarity:** use descriptive citation labels and authored Arabic geographic context. Identify an area reference as such when it differs from the client's requested residence type. Never recast an apartment reference as a villa match.
3. **Brief precision:** make individual fields editable, expose British-school/children qualifiers, distinguish ready-before from a generic requested year, and keep multi-market alternatives. Stage3 must turn the illustrative teaching state into an actual grounded city explanation.
4. **Mobile comfort:** increase support-text comfort, use status-line guidance rather than overlapping toasts, and retain accessible keyboard sheet controls alongside drag behavior.
5. **Selected identity:** create a generous selected-project treatment and zoom-aware map-label hierarchy. Keep one composition rather than introducing a generic results grid.

These items are assigned to the relevant later implementation stages because they are production design obligations or residual polish above a passed concept average. No mandatory later feature is waived. Stage6's stricter final review/performance requirements remain open.
