## Three delights

1. The financial comparison preserves the actual native price basis and does not turn a developer's target delivery into a guarantee.
2. A useful Brief can precede advisor contact. The validation covers permission boundaries and atomic confirmations; the UI receipt test explicitly simulates its response rather than claiming a real handoff.
3. Reference projects, fictional inventory and unknown evidence are distinguished visibly. This is a better basis for brand trust than invented availability.

## Three problems

1. **Make location qualification explicit.** Resolve the Palm ambiguity and popup geography, or label a genuine out-of-area option as such. “Compare with your requested areas” is not an explanation of fit.
2. **Put the decision content first.** [Mobile Karim](../media/v2/karim-en-dark-390.png) shows introductory material while the project stays below the fold. Consolidate repetitive notices without dropping their substance.
3. **Improve usable evidence density.** Remove the duplicate original-price row or present its qualification legibly, and repair the partly hidden mobile zoom targets. Both obstruct assessment rather than merely decorating the page.

The [execution evidence](../validation/execution-passing-v3.json) has zero reported safety counters against seeded data; it is not current commercial verification or real-provider testing. The matrix records zero runtime errors/overflow but sixteen axe failures. The fresh Lighthouse accessibility score of 100 does not override those state-specific findings. Current-source CI failed its Production Lighthouse step. Native Arabic/AT, M1/physical-phone, field INP and conversion evidence remain open.

**Verdict: Revise before passing—the source discipline is persuasive, but recommendation geography and mobile assessment need to become unambiguous.**
