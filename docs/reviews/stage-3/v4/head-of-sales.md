## Three problems

1. **Meet the loading budget.** The final lab result still exceeds two seconds. High Lighthouse performance and low blocking time do not cancel that separate requirement.
2. **Phone financial reading remains dense.** The [SouthMED price view](../media/v7-currency-final/karim-currencies/ar-390-usd-prices.png) still needs several compact lines for native basis, conversion, date and source. These are necessary facts, but comparison remains less immediately scannable than the desktop presentation.
3. **Commercial verification remains a handoff boundary.** An honest source-basis unknown and missing schedule are preferable to invention, but they cannot qualify an executable purchase or current availability. The demo must retain that boundary in actual advisor use; I am not treating later-stage sales tooling as absent Stage 3 work.
