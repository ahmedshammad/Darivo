## Evidence policy

Screenshots and videos come from current Playwright runs and live prototypes. Simulated persona reviews are labeled as such and cite specific captures/moments. No automatic blanket 10s. Passing a prototype review does not certify production functionality, performance, native linguistic quality or legal compliance. Unverified external gates remain open; they are never described as passed because local substitutes passed.

## Current external dependencies

Reference dataset located and copied without alteration. The supplied GitHub destination is configured; Stage2 source c5033b8 and its hosted run39824920382 are green. A temporary native PostgreSQL16+pgvector fixture supports local migration, seed, unit and execution-eval checks. Docker itself remains unavailable locally. Browser recognition does not establish bilingual ASR auto-detection or dialect acoustic accuracy; a consented multilingual speech adapter remains the proposed alternative. Native Gulf-Arabic editorial, assistive-technology, physical-phone/M1 and field-performance validation remain distinct from local automated or simulated evidence. Current Stage3 source has not yet passed hosted CI.
