# Stage 2 — Motion Designer

Karim's change from North Coast to Dubai should feel like a change of place, not a reset. The map stops an interrupted flight before accepting a new camera key. Default flights last 600ms with a gentle deceleration. Reduced-motion users receive the same destination and information immediately. No breathing glows, indefinite spin, repeated auto-flight or bounce.

Arrival begins with Gulf and Mediterranean geography, then follows an explicit parent camera request. A restrained initial approach may happen once after load; touching the map cancels it. The globe and local camera use the same real basemap. Viewer-local time affects the warm/cool ambient lighting, with no claim that it predicts property sunlight.

Layer changes are immediate and labels remain stable. Project elevation is a data encoding rather than a decorative entrance; any interpolation is disabled under reduced motion. The renderer caps device pixels and avoids continuous animation when the view is still. Parent stories supply sequenced camera keys and captions, keeping pause/skip under the user's control.

Validation required: capture intent → camera → selection at 1440/1024/390 in both languages; measure frame intervals during a flight, confirm no camera travel with reduced motion, and confirm selection is above the mobile sheet. Do not claim 60fps before measurement.
