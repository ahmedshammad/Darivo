# Creative direction decision — Stage 0, iteration 1

## Chosen direction: Noir Atlas

Use Concept C's revised globe arrival and conversation/map continuity as the structural direction, with Concept A's Noir/Sand restraint and Concept B's evidence typography. This is a selection for refinement, not a passed review or permission to start Stage 1. Concept C remains the selected prototype and keeps its versioned evidence.

## Why this direction

The current captures show three distinct approaches. A makes a strong cinematic coastal arrival but lets oversized copy compete with the selected place and has Arabic/mobile defects. B offers the best reading structure and fictional-price disclosure, but turns the map into an editorial panel and pushes it below the first mobile screen. C v2 gives the two markets a spatial relationship immediately, keeps the composer visible and retains a continuous map/brief context after submission. That directly answers the brief's central proposition.

## Disagreements

- Creative Director favours A's immediate atmosphere; Product Designer prefers B's reading order. Decision: keep atmosphere in the geographic canvas, use editorial structure inside contextual panels, and avoid recreating a list page.
- AI Interaction Designer favours C's visible Brief; Accessibility Lead rejects overlays that cover the active map target. Decision: camera padding must reflect visible sheets and panels.
- Data Lead rejects impressive but unlinked numbers. Decision: add one clearly separated reference spotlight with original source, basis and date; demo concepts stay fictional and never inherit real project availability.

## Required fixes before re-review

1. Remove map label clutter behind titles, navigation and the globe; show only deliberately selected geographic labels at arrival scale.
2. Apply the requested Noir/Sand palette, rather than a teal technology dashboard.
3. Give Arabic editorial text a deliberate un-slanted treatment, correct initial labels, readable text and comfortable wrapping at390.
4. Keep the selected map location above the mobile sheet and between desktop panels; make the mobile sheet keyboard-operable and expandable.
5. Show a concise source/date/basis reference card without suggesting real availability. Add an honest recommendation trade-off.
6. Keep attribution legible and physically separated from the composer/footer at all widths.
7. Verify the chosen typed-intent journey in both languages, keyboard operation, reduced motion, axe and the complete screenshot/recording matrix.

## Gate status

Open. The initial reviews identify material defects; no score is rounded up to satisfy the gate. The team will re-review the selected, repaired concept before any production feature work.
