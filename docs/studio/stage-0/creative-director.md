# Creative Director — Stage 0

## Taste thesis

Darivo should feel like being welcomed into a private property office overlooking a city, rather than entering a catalogue. The city is the first image. A carefully worded question is the first action. Typography supplies the character that licensed imagery cannot yet supply.

## Concept A: Noir Cinematic

The map fills the viewport. A quiet, warm black scrim allows oversized Cormorant lettering to coexist with the geography. Bronze is a punctuation mark. A small live location readout supplies orientation. The arrival transitions into a more useful, equally restrained map workspace: a conversation rail and one selected-place dossier, not a grid. Every example is labelled Demo.

The two alternatives must differ structurally, not merely in colour: an editorial atlas and an instrument-like spatial studio are worthwhile counterarguments. Selection remains pending screenshot review; this brief does not award itself a score.

## Acceptance bar

- At 390px, the primary question and submit action remain reachable without obscuring the geographic subject.
- English and Arabic have independently composed line lengths and hierarchy.
- Light theme receives its own ink, paper and map treatment.
- Selected-place content shows an honest trade-off and a Demo basis before any transaction action.
- No fake market numbers or unsourced claims about a real developer.

## Disagreement to resolve

A slow arrival flight could establish atmosphere but delay the first useful action. The interaction designer should win here: input is usable immediately, and camera motion never gates navigation. Map attribution stays visible even when it challenges the cinematic frame.

## Scope and evidence

This is a static concept sprint only. Authentication, AI reasoning, trust records, map terrain, production performance and private data protection are not implemented or claimed by these prototypes. Final concept choice and review synthesis follow actual captures of all three directions.
