# Principal Product Designer · Stage 0

## Product thesis

Darivo starts with a life decision, not an inventory transaction. The first useful action is a sentence. The next useful state is a place, with a short explanation of why it belongs in the conversation. A result is a recommendation with an honest trade-off, not a tile with an enquiry button.

## Persona-led entry flows

- Sheikha opens Arabic, describes a private family home, sees her brief stay on screen, then chooses a discreet introduction. Privacy language must describe real behavior; no badge claiming encryption or verification without implementation.
- Karim starts with two markets. The map makes the difference in place clear; prices retain their original currency and a conversion needs a dated rate.
- Elena asks what living in Dubai would mean. Collections act as introductory spatial stories before inventory.
- Rajiv opens the evidence before the image. Data basis, date and source belong beside every consequential number.
- A developer sees a place and an architectural idea receive room to breathe, rather than competing with a grid of agents.
- An advisor receives the editable client brief and unresolved questions, not a raw transcript alone.

## Sand Editorial proposal

A magazine cover opens into an atlas. Typography, an asymmetric division of space, fine rules and a large geographic map create the composition. The map occupies the majority of the Explore state. A persistent conversation composer connects the two states; it never becomes an unrelated chat destination. Desktop uses an editorial margin for a small selection. Mobile uses an explicitly expandable sheet, without obscuring all the geography.

The prototype tests home → describe an intent → selected region on map → inspect a fictional recommendation → save locally. Home and Explore are views in one document so a reviewer can assess the transition without backend implementation. Cities and collection prompts move the camera; controls display their actual prototype limitations.

## Information hierarchy

1. What kind of life are you considering?
2. Where could that life happen?
3. Why this place, what is the compromise, and what is the evidence?
4. Save privately or invite a human advisor.

Keep primary destination changes within two actions. Make return-to-map, editing the brief, and changing language persistent. The production conversation is not allowed to create irreversible actions without a clear user action.

## Disagreement and decision

The full-screen cinematic-map direction maximizes arrival drama. This proposal gives more space to language and typography, which may reduce immediate spectacle but increases differentiation from a map-search portal. Do not select the winner by taste alone: compare recorded entry flows at all requested viewports and languages. Stage 0 scores remain pending captured evidence.

## Risks and acceptance evidence

Static concepts cannot prove retention, real personalization, privacy enforcement, frame rate on target phones, or native Gulf language quality. Inspect screenshot matrices for line breaks and map balance; record keyboard and touch flows; run axe; request native-reader review before claiming native-quality Arabic. Production constraints and proof belong in later stage gates.
