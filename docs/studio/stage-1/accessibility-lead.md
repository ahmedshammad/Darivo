# Accessibility Lead · Stage 1

Display preferences use native labelled controls and status announcements. Numeral choice is explicit, keyboard-operable and demonstrated with a preview; it is not an unlabeled icon. Saving failure must be visible and cannot be mistaken for persistence. Changing a preference does not navigate unexpectedly or move focus.

Role boundaries are functional accessibility too: users receive the appropriate gate rather than an empty private-data screen. New partner and verified-client identities have distinct, translated demo labels. Public browsing remains available independently of sign-in.

Use semantic bidi isolation for amounts, not visual reversal. Decimal/currency values must remain parseable by assistive technology and independent of locale. Test formatting for both number systems, original currencies and indicative conversions. Test server authorization directly, including stale identity snapshots, role downgrade, verification downgrade and a partner with an old private grant.

Stage 1 review will combine automated checks with keyboard and screenshot inspection across EN/AR themes and breakpoints. Zero axe issues is necessary evidence, not a claim of complete WCAG 2.2 AA conformance or physical-device performance.
