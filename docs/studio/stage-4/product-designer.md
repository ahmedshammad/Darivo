# Principal Product Designer — Project Story discovery

The story must help someone decide what to examine next, not force a buyer through a long branded article. The editorial sequence and proposed contract are in [STORY-PROPOSAL](STORY-PROPOSAL.md). This role output is design-only; no implementation or Stage 4 acceptance is claimed.

## Persona-led decisions

- **Elena:** arrive from the Concierge with the selected place and family Brief intact. “The place” names relevant areas before activating layers. School examples retain Demo/unverified status; the story does not turn approximate proximity into school suitability. Her unresolved Stage 3 need for concise lifestyle tradeoffs should be met only by supported area facts or clearly posed questions for the advisor, never invented reassurance.
- **Karim:** native price and a chosen display currency stay separate from intended payment currency. A source-qualified EGP starting figure and a complete Demo-unit schedule cannot share an unlabeled “payment plan” treatment. Numbers has a visible subject identity even when the earlier hero has scrolled away.
- **Sheikha:** Arabic begins with a calm named place, an explicit availability state and discretion. Asking about residences is a draft enquiry, not consent to contact or proof of beachfront inventory. Exact source wording is available, while faithful Arabic display text handles the primary reading.
- **Head of Sales/Rajiv:** chronology and numbers have stated scope. Sourced project positioning is welcome; invented completion certainty, yield or a developer-quality score is not. Native price basis/date remains alongside converted numbers and in the dossier.

## Information hierarchy

Use one h1 for the exact subject, then h2 for Idea, Place, Residences, Numbers, Developer, Service and Next steps; generated facts inside use h3 as needed. Chapter navigation is a labelled nav with anchors, not eight tabs that remove content. Default text is visible in server HTML. Original source wording/calculation can use native details; facts needed to understand a decision cannot be hidden there.

On desktop, the media rail provides continuity while the text advances. On phone, prioritize name, location, kind, availability and an action before decorative height. Keep one document scroll. Evidence notes use contextual note semantics, not repeated complementary landmarks; repeated small scroll containers are groups. Retain unique heading/description IDs. Focus moves only following an explicit action, and anchor offsets account for header/action bar.

## Action hierarchy

“Ask about this” is always a contextual Concierge draft, never an automatic request. Primary action follows authorized subject kind: real reference → enquire about residences; demo project → choose a residence; demo unit → request viewing. Add to Portfolio and Compare remain direct secondary actions. A saved receipt names both exact subject and portfolio, with a link to the next step. The portfolio then exposes Dossier without another search.

Do not silently migrate a real-project reference into a synthetic unit. Do not claim a grouping project is owned stock. A reference may be saved as a research interest; unit holdings and reference interests keep their kind labels throughout comparison and PDF. Auth return restores draft/context without triggering a POST. Revoked or unavailable subjects remove action capabilities, explain the state without disclosing hidden details, and preserve other valid portfolio items.

## Existing grounding and reuse

Inspected [Arabic reference card](../../reviews/stage-3/media/v7/sheikha-refined-ar-light-390.png) establishes the right proximity of a caveat to price; [named timeline](../../reviews/stage-3/media/v8-timeline-final/timeline-en-dark-1440.png) establishes identity independent of earlier content. Reuse shared formatting/preferences, grounded fact/source structures and timeline semantics. Design-system components are useful visual specimens, not ready-made authorization or production data adapters. Legacy property-detail UI is not a suitable generic wrapper for source-only real projects.

## Disagreement and decision

An uninterrupted eight-chapter story would show the brand beautifully but slow the required three-minute path. Keep the full article and add obvious chapter shortcuts and contextual actions. A large phone sticky image would preserve atmosphere but consume reading space; use normal-flow media on phone and keep the action bar compact. “Private sharing” sounds attractive, but a copied private route is not a controlled share; require a real revocable permission model before offering that claim.

Acceptance should verify real-reference unknown states as carefully as the positive Demo viewing flow. The under-three-minute recording is a seeded-client usability test with visible elapsed time, not a promise about email delivery, advisor response or a real booking.
