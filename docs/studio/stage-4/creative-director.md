# Creative Director — Stage 4

Start with Karim carrying an Egypt/Dubai comparison into a private decision, and Sheikha checking what a saved item will reveal. Their question is what deserves further attention, not how to browse a larger catalogue. The stage continues the accepted Noir Atlas: geographic media, high-contrast editorial type, warm light mode, bronze used for focus/evidence, generous reading rhythm and co-designed Arabic.

The Project Story must read as an architectural research note: identity and basis first; then the idea, place, residences, numbers, developer, service and next steps. Use the actual living map and the existing licensed/local visual system; do not invent developer imagery or a building's form. For reference projects, every narrative claim must be traceable to a dataset fact. An unknown fact can be a deliberately concise absence; it must never become decorative filler. Reference-project residences are available on request, not fictional available stock.

The Private Office is a small collection of decisions: named portfolio boards, a comparison, a sourced dossier, requested viewings and a private advisor conversation. It must not resemble an operational dashboard full of unrelated metrics. The next action is explicit and persistent; client content is private by default. A fictional advisor identity is clearly demo, and presence means an authored availability state with a timestamp, not simulated live activity.

Maintain one visual language between Concierge, map, story and office. The recorded acceptance path uses a real authorized demo unit for the viewing step, while reference interests demonstrate source-backed on-request handling separately. The target is under three minutes for the complete demonstrated path, with the automation scope stated rather than presented as human usability proof.

Review against all seven personas and12 criteria. Preserve failed rounds. Broad axe and actual screenshots precede claims of craft; measurements remain scoped to device and method. Loading gaps from Stage3 are tracked, not erased by a new page.
